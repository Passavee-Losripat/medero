## Fonts and assets

- Demo video background music: by **AtlasAudio** from **Pixabay**
  (https://pixabay.com/users/atlasaudio-54514918/, Pixabay item 613514), used under the Pixabay Content License
  (free use, attribution not required; credited in the video description). Only in the demo video, not in the app.

- MOCK prescription images in `data/mock/prescriptions/` (labels and 처방전 forms, clean and simulated phone
  photos) are rendered by `data/mock/render_mock_docs.py` with **Noto Sans KR** (print) and **Nanum Pen Script**
  (handwritten note), both from google/fonts under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. The fonts are downloaded at
  render time into `data/mock/.fonts/` (not committed); only the rendered MOCK images are. Re-rendered 2026-11-05
  (earlier renders used macOS system fonts and were replaced).
- Medero logo (`assets/brand/medero-logo-source.png`; derived icons in `web/public/`): generated by the
  developer with ChatGPT (OpenAI) on 2026-11-04 for this project. OpenAI's terms assign output rights to the
  user. Used only as a brand asset; no OpenAI model is part of Medero's runtime.
- Welcome illustration (`web/components/Hero.tsx`) and icons (`web/components/Icon.tsx`): original SVG, made for this project.
- All case cards, utterances, role-plays, prescriptions, receipts and insurer texts in
  `data/mock/` are invented for this project and labelled MOCK. No scraped or copied text.
